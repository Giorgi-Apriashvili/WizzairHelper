Wizz Air (https://wizzair.com)
    Fares and timetables come from the public website's unofficial API. Wizz Air
    is a trademark of its owner. Prices are indicative; book on wizzair.com.

Photon (https://photon.komoot.io, by komoot) and Nominatim
(https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org) - geocoding of the places you type.
    Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
    License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
    Nominatim is used within its usage policy (at most one request per second,
    an identifying User-Agent, results cached locally).

Frankfurter (https://frankfurter.dev) and currency-api by Fawaz Ahmed
(https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api, via jsDelivr and Cloudflare)
    Exchange rates for converting prices. Frankfurter publishes rates from the
    European Central Bank and other sources; currency-api is published under
